BEFORE YOU START

How to use the damage checker

  1. Record two different videos: one before and one after inspection.
  2. Keep each video under 5 minutes and show the vehicle throughout.
  3. Use the same vehicle in both videos and keep it visible throughout.
  4. Do not include people, animals, or unrelated objects. Use good lighting and a steady camera angle.
  5. After both videos pass AI checks, move the slider to compare a moment, then run the analysis.
  6. Capture the evidence frame and download the PDF report if a possible change is found.

AI results are visual guidance only. Always confirm scratches or damage manually before making a claim or decision.
